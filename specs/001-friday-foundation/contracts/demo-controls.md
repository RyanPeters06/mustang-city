# Demo controls (laptop only)

Press **D** (not while typing in an input) to toggle a small panel outside the phone frame.

| Control | Effect |
|---|---|
| Walk campus | Feeds a scripted path of positions across campus into the reveal pipeline at ~6 fixes/s; camera follows |
| Reveal here | Reveals the cell at the map centre + ring 1 |
| Fly to Mustang City | Eases camera to campus at z16 (triggers 3D mode) |
| Fly to Forest State | Eases camera to the city overview |
| Reset fog | Clears revealed cells back to the default campus reveal |

All controls go through the same functions as live GPS (`revealAt`).
